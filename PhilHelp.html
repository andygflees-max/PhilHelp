Welcome to PhilHelp

An independent tool to help you find official Philippine government programs, services, rights, and procedures. Answers cite *.gov.ph sources and suggest next steps.

Important: PhilHelp is not affiliated with the Philippine government. It does not process applications, issue IDs, or accept payments. Use it only to locate the correct official resource, then complete any transaction directly with the agency or LGU.
